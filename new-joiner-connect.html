The problem

People leave before we ever learn why

Over the last three financial years, a steady share of new joiners left within their first 90 days. Each early exit wastes sourcing effort, time and onboarding investment.

Role fit

The job turns out different from what was described in the offer.

Manager connect

No early bond with the reporting manager or team.

Systems

Tools, access and processes are confusing in the first weeks.

Culture & belonging

The joiner never quite feels part of the team.

These are our likely causes — but they go unheard, because people leave before anyone asks.

New Joiner Connect: a 60-minute session with HR

Why Day 15–20?

It's the quiet valley. The first wave of exits (Day 0–7) has passed and the second wave (Day 23–30) hasn't started — people can still be influenced.

Touchpoint 3 · Day 40–45

A one-on-one HR call once joiners have settled in

Why Day 40–45?

Late enough that joiners have real experience to share about their role, manager and systems.

Why it matters

Focusing only on the first 30 days could push exits into the next period. This call is a preventive safety net.

How it works

  • One-on-one, not a group. More candid — HR calls each joiner personally.
  • Four areas to explore: challenges, support received, team belonging, role clarity.
  • Themes go to action owners: recurring issues are routed to Recruitment, L&D or managers and tracked until closed.
Way forward

One purpose: hear every new joiner and act on it

01

Run the sessions

A "Hi-Tea with HR" group connect at Day 15–20 for every new joiner.

02

Gather the data

4–5 sessions reveal a pattern: likes, dislikes, one-word experiences.

03

Find the gaps

Where is support missing: manager connect, role clarity, systems or belonging?

04

Act to reduce

Route findings to Recruitment, L&D or managers, and track them until closed.

After 4–5 sessions, the data tells us where the system is letting new joiners down. That is where we intervene — and that is how this moves the attrition number.
