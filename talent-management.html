What great looks like

Eight leadership competencies, aligned to the new vision

Customer Centricity

Puts customer needs first to deliver exceptional experiences and value.

Business Acumen

Understands finance and the market, and makes strategic decisions.

Visionary Leadership

Drives transformational growth and anticipates industry trends.

People Leadership

Develops, nurtures and empowers high-performing teams.

Data Mindset

Uses analytics to drive decisions and build an edge.

Rigour in Execution

Ensures high-quality, disciplined delivery of key initiatives.

Emotional Intelligence

Shows self-awareness, empathy and relationship-building skills.

Future Mindset

Adapts to trends, embraces change and prepares for market shifts.

Three proficiency levels

Each competency grows from Basic to Expert

Two examples of how expectations rise with seniority.

People Leadership

Basic

Provides guidance and supports team development.

Intermediate

Empowers teams, delegates well and drives accountability.

Expert

Develops future leaders and builds a high-performance culture.

Customer Centricity

Basic

Listens to what customers ask for and acts on the feedback.

Intermediate

Engages customers across channels to uncover unspoken needs and pain points.

Expert

Builds organisation-wide customer-first strategies.
