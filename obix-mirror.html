The problem

We know what people say — not how they act under pressure

CVs & interviews

Show what people say they would do — usually the textbook answer.

Long psychometric tests

Feel like exams, take time, and people "game" them.

What we need

A quick, engaging way to see everyday habits — the real baseline for coaching.

How it works

What each person gets

A personal blueprint in under 3 minutes

Hero archetype

The Spark, The Anvil, The Sentinel or The Keystone — easy to remember and share.

Thinker level

Low-level (reactive) or high-level (systems) thinker, with a gauge.

Consciousness level

"You are here" on the 7-level ladder, plus their growth edge.

Leadership level

Their Barrett leadership type and readiness: Emerging → Strong Potential.

Blind spot

Their weakest thinking pillar, explained simply.

7-day rule

One IF–THEN habit to practise this week.

What HR gets

Every response, organised automatically

Google Sheet log — name, role, scores, levels and every choice, colour-coded.

Live summary — high vs low thinkers, level spread, leadership readiness by role.

Zero cost to run — one HTML page on GitHub Pages plus Google Apps Script.

Where HR can use it

  • Onboarding — a self-awareness baseline in week 1
  • L&D needs analysis — see blind spots across teams
  • Leadership pipeline — a first read on readiness
  • Coaching — a conversation starter for managers
  • Employer branding — a shareable experience
Used responsibly

A mirror, not a verdict

Not a hiring decision

Eight self-chosen answers can't judge ability. Use it alongside interviews and validated assessments.

Transparent consent

People are told their responses are recorded, and can skip the form and still see their results.

Built for growth

Every result ends with a concrete next step, not a label.
