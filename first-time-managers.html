Why this program

Promoted for performance — now leading people

First-time managers connect strategy, teams and customers. Most were promoted for their individual results, and they tend to struggle in three ways.

Still doing, not managing. They keep working as doers instead of managing outcomes.

Authority vs friendship. It's hard to balance authority with relationships — often with former peers.

Workshops fade. A one-off workshop builds awareness, but behaviour only changes with practice over time.

~4 in 10

of this year's promotions take on people-management responsibility for the first time — now or in the near future.

Hence a 6-month learn → apply → review journey.

Learning design

Every month runs the same four-step cycle

Learning sticks when it is practised.

Week 1

Learn

One 8-hour classroom session: concepts, cases, simulations and role plays.

Weeks 1–4

Apply

On-the-job assignments with their real team, using the month's toolkit.

Week 3

Reflect

A 60-minute peer learning circle to share what worked and what didn't.

Week 4

Review

A 30-minute check-in with the reporting manager, then a share-back at the next session.

At a glance

The 6-month roadmap

Month 1Lead Self

Transitioning to People Leadership

Tools: DISC · Johari Window · Leadership Canvas

On the job: DISC workbook, a weekly doer-to-manager journal, goals agreed with your manager

Month 2Lead Self

Managing Self, Time and Work

Tools: Eisenhower Matrix · Situational Leadership · RACI

On the job: One-week time audit, delegate two real tasks, a RACI for one project

Month 3Lead Team

Communication, Trust and Feedback

Tools: Trust Equation · SBI feedback · Conflict modes (TKI)

On the job: Team trust audit, three SBI feedback talks, one avoided conversation

Month 4Lead Team

Coaching and Performance

Tools: SMART goals · GROW model · Accountability ladder

On the job: SMART goals for every team member, two GROW coaching talks

Month 5Lead Business

Problem Solving and Business Acumen

Tools: 5 Whys · Fishbone · Decision matrix · Stakeholder map

On the job: Link the capstone to a unit metric, a decision journal, a stakeholder map

Month 6Lead Business

High-Performing Teams and Capstone

Tools: Tuckman · Belbin team roles · Team health check

On the job: Team health check, a 90-day leadership plan, post-program 360

Measuring effectiveness

Four levels, from reaction to results

Level 1

Reaction

Feedback after every session — target 4 or more out of 5

Level 2

Learning

Pre and post knowledge checks, plus the quality of assignments

Level 3

Behaviour

Pre and post 180° feedback from manager, peers and team; manager observations in months 3 and 6

Level 4

Results

Team attrition, customer escalations, goal achievement, engagement pulse, capstone evidence

Certification: attend at least 5 of 6 sessions, complete 80% of assignments and present the capstone.
